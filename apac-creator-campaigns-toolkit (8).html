A creator is someone who has built their own audience by publishing original content either video, photo or audio on social platforms like TikTok, Instagram or YouTube.

Unlike a traditional celebrity influencer endorsement, the value is further than just reach: it's the trust and engagement a creator has earned with people who chose to follow them which makes their content convert.

Nano*
Under 10K followers. Small, tightly engaged niche audience.
Micro
10K–100K followers. Strong engagement rates, accessible cost.
Mid-tier
100K–500K followers. Established audience and professional content.
Macro
500K–1M followers. Broad reach with a proven, brand-safe track record.
Mega / Celebrity
1M+ followers. Mass reach, often paired with existing fame off-platform.

*Not available with Live Nation.

These tiers are general industry rules of thumb, not a fixed standard — ranges vary by platform and market. See the Creator Directory tab for real rates by tier.

Paid media alone buys attention; it doesn't buy trust. A creator brings an audience that already listens to them, content that doesn't look like an ad, and a faster route into markets and communities a brand doesn't have a foothold in yet.

Credibility
Fans trust a creator's recommendation more than a brand's own ad.
Content that performs
Native-feeling video and photo consistently out-engages traditional ad creative.
Access
Reaches audiences a brand can't build itself — local, niche or younger fanbases.
Speed
Content goes live in days, not the weeks a traditional production needs.

Booking creator and media through Live Nation buys a guaranteed, measured outcome, with one party accountable for all of it.

Rates and relationships already exist
Talent fees, travel costs and management terms per market are already negotiated. A partner going direct starts from zero and pays retail.
One point of accountability
Talent, media and reporting sit under a single contract and a single team — if a creator underdelivers, that's Live Nation's problem to fix, not the client's.
Reach is guaranteed
Direct-to-creator gets organic reach only. Bundling in Live Nation media backs the content with paid distribution, so impressions are a number in the contract, not a hope.
Targeting the creator can't do alone
A creator's own following is one audience and the partner's is their own audience. Live Nation layers a fresh new audience to find new prospects whilst engaging at scale.

The management fee isn't a markup on the creator — it's what pays for the guarantee, the targeting and the accountability a direct booking doesn't come with.

Not every campaign needs one. Creators earn their spot when the goal is trust, reach into a specific audience, or content at a volume and speed paid media can't produce alone.

New audience
Targeting an untested audience — or even genre, market, demographics.
Launch moment
A tour, artist or event announcement that needs a fast, credible spike in attention.
Proof over polish
The message lands better as lived experience than as a produced ad.
Content gap
The media plan needs more creative volume than the production budget allows.

If none of these apply, paid media on its own may do the job — creators add cost and lead time, so they're worth it when they're solving one of the problems above.
